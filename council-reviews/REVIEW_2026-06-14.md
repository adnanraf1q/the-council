## Executive summary

**Verdict: invest.** The project provides a solid framework for managing and distributing portable instructions. However, the recent removal of finding limits is a major step backward, transforming a strategic advisory panel into a wordy, expensive linter. Restoring finding caps is the highest-priority action, followed by end-to-end CI tests to catch prompt regressions and adding data-privacy warnings.

### The Critic (no filter)

We've built a CI pipeline to lint the configuration file of a tool that does absolutely nothing but read files and output markdown. By removing the finding limits, we've transformed what was a focused, high-level advisory board into an expensive, wordy linter. The council no longer curates its feedback; it just vomits every minor observation it can find into a file, inflating the user's token bill and wasting their time. If we wanted a tool that lists fifty minor styling issues, we would use ESLint for free, not a multi-million-parameter LLM that charges per token. We need to decide whether the council is an executive board or a noisy junior developer.
