The anti-drift mission is now generating drift — three copies of the role prose and two unsynchronized version numbers — which is a special kind of irony for a repo named after a panel of experts. And the headline feature of this session is making the robot tell jokes. None of it is wrong, exactly, but the council has spent another session grooming itself instead of judging anyone. The version string says 1.2.0; the number of external apps reviewed says 0. Fix the caps, wire the version gate, then close the laptop and run it on something that isn't itself.
