### The Critic (no filter)

The council has become its own best customer and its own worst finding. The repo's product is a prompt, and that prompt grew 2.7× in ten days while the number of documented external reviews stayed at zero — the same trajectory every prior Critic paragraph warned about, now with better tooling. The infrastructure is no longer the embarrassment; it's actually good — CI catches real drift, past actions genuinely got fixed, the discipline is visible in the git log. The embarrassment is that v1.5 through v1.9 read like a security-blog wishlist stapled onto the role bullets, not like scar tissue from real reviews, and there is no changelog that could prove otherwise. Meanwhile the skill now violates its own checks: unchecked file growth (Architect), no CLAUDE.md (Reliability), an unhandled injection surface (Security). The 06-12 Critic said the only review that counts is the one of an app that isn't this repo; three weeks and seven versions later, that sentence has survived four reviews untouched. Make v2.0 the release where the diff is net-negative lines and the changelog cites someone else's codebase.
